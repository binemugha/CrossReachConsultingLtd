Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and approach

Who are your affiliates and partners?

CrossReach Consulting and its Associates have provided technical assistance to a diverse range of partners, including international donor-funded projects, government agencies, and private organizations. Notable collaborators include USAID and its implementing partners (Pact Incorporated, Capacity Plus, Management Sciences for Health, MEASURE Evaluation), United Nations bodies (UNICEF, UNAIDS, UNDP), and various Ministries, Departments, and Agencies including FMWASD, NACA, NTI, and NPoPC.

What sectors do you work with?

We engage in technical assistance across multiple sectors, spanning international development, public health, education, social welfare, and private industry. Our collaborative efforts extend to governmental entities involved in social development and healthcare, as well as private sector partners. This diverse portfolio underscores our commitment to addressing multifaceted challenges through tailored technical support and capacity building.

How do you contribute to capacity building?

Through partnerships with USAID, United Nations bodies, and governmental agencies, we provide technical assistance aimed at strengthening the effectiveness and sustainability of interventions. This includes support for research initiatives, evaluation studies, and capacity-building programs designed to empower stakeholders and institutions involved in public health, education, and social development.

What makes CrossReach Consulting different?

We combine international standards of professionalism with deep local context understanding. Our partners bring decades of hands-on experience in the Nigerian and African development sectors. We don't just deliver reports — we build lasting capacities, transfer skills, and ensure our partners can sustain improvements long after our engagement ends.

Where is your office located?

Our head office is located at If My People House, 36, Alao Akala Way, Akobo, Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria. We work with clients across Nigeria and throughout Africa, and our consultants are available for field assignments as needed.
